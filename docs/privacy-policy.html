PiyoPiyo Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026. PiyoPiyo is a computer-science practice app maintained by Francisco Cruzado Arroyo.

Practice and accounts

You can practice without an account. Answers, drafts and progress are saved on your device. Signing in uses Amazon Cognito and, if selected, Sign in with Apple. Your email, account identifier and any name supplied during sign-in support your account. Signing in moves guest progress into your account. Closed-question answers and progress sync to PiyoPiyo’s dedicated AWS services in the United States.

Optional AI feedback

AI feedback is off by default for each account. When enabled in Settings, saved and future written answers are sent to PiyoPiyo’s AWS backend and Amazon Bedrock for rubric-based feedback. Avoid including passwords, confidential information or personal details in answers. Disabling AI feedback stops future written-answer uploads; it does not delete answers already synced. AI grades can be incorrect and are for practice, not official assessment.

Study rooms

Room members can see your chosen display name and score. Collaborative rooms also share submitted answers with members. Only share a room code with people you want to join. Rooms close after two hours; physical deletion may occur later through AWS expiration processing.

Diagnostics and tracking

Optional diagnostics send aggregate session, render-failure and queue counts when enabled. Service logs contain operational events, errors and model latency; the application does not intentionally log answer text or authentication secrets. Operational logs expire after seven days. PiyoPiyo includes no advertising or cross-app tracking.

Retention and deletion

Progress remains until you erase it or delete your account. Signing out retains local progress, separated by account. Settings lets you erase local data or permanently delete your cloud account and its progress and room contributions. Apple accounts reauthorize to revoke PiyoPiyo’s Apple access. A temporary identifier blocks old sign-in tokens for up to 24 hours after deletion. Database backups expire within seven days. Offline copies on other devices must be erased there too.

Choices and contact

You can use guest practice, disable AI feedback or diagnostics, sign out, erase local progress or delete your account in Settings. Contact fcruzado22@gmail.com for privacy or support requests. Changes to this policy will be reflected by its effective date.

PiyoPiyo Sources and background learning

Content administrators can submit PDFs, text files, and public links through PiyoPiyo Sources. The app asks for consent before uploading. Originals, extracted passages, and review evidence are stored in private, app-owned AWS storage. Selected passages are sent to Amazon Bedrock in US regions to generate and review original questions. Source titles and public URLs may appear in learner citations; original uploaded files and evidence excerpts are not sent to learners.

Sources and batch records persist as the app's content library; operational jobs expire after 90 days and logs after seven days. Pausing a source or withdrawing a batch does not erase its archived content. Learner account deletion removes learner progress and identity as described above; it does not delete the separately administered shared content library. Contact the administrator to manage retained source material. The learning pipeline does not retrain model weights or use learner answers for question generation.
