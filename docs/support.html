Saved progress

Your answers save on your device before syncing. If sync is unavailable, reconnect and open the app again. Cloud feedback on written answers requires sign-in and AI feedback enabled in Settings. Provisional feedback may remain pending while the grading service is unavailable or its daily limit is reached.

Accounts and rooms

Use Settings to sign in, sign out or delete an account. If deletion is interrupted, reconnect and choose Delete account again. Study room codes expire after two hours.

Safe study rooms

Keep study rooms respectful. Harassment, hateful or sexual content, threats, spam, and sharing another person's private information are not allowed. Display names are screened for common abusive words and links; room answers are limited to the question's supplied choices.

Open the safety menu beside a participant to report harassment, inappropriate content, spam, or another concern. Reports include the participant's name and recent shared contributions and are visible only to the administrator. Choose Block and leave room to leave immediately and prevent either account from joining a room containing the other. You can continue private practice.

Reports are reviewed by the app administrator, who can restrict room access and remove the participant from room listings. Contact fcruzado22@gmail.com for urgent concerns or to appeal a restriction. Do not send passwords.
